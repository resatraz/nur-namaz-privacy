Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Overview

Secde Pro (formerly Nur Namaz Programı) is developed by Mehmet Reşat Raz. This page explains, in clear language, what information is handled, why it is processed, and how you can delete your data.

No account is required to use the app, and we never ask for your name, email address, or phone number.

2. Information Collected and Processed

a) Location

Location access is used only while the app is open to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. Your coordinates are processed on your device; only the selected city or coordinates are sent to the prayer-time providers listed in Section 5, solely to fetch prayer times. Location is not stored on our servers.

b) Preferences and local data

Your selected city, theme, font size, and notification/prayer-tracking preferences are stored only on your device (SharedPreferences). The home screen widget stores the same prayer-time information (city name and times) locally in the app's shared container; it is never sent to the internet.

c) Notification permission

Used for adhan-time reminders, daily verse, and religious holiday notifications. Notifications are produced on your device; we do not report which notifications you receive.

d) Prayer Request feature (user-generated content)

If you voluntarily submit a prayer request, the text, the username you choose (optionally "Anonymous"), and an anonymous device identifier are stored in Google Firebase Firestore so other users can view it and say "Amen." This content is published with your consent and can be deleted at any time (see Section 7).

e) Crash reports

Firebase Crashlytics collects anonymous crash reports (OS version, error logs, device model, and similar technical details) to detect and fix bugs. These reports contain no personally identifiable information linked to you.

f) Advertisements

The app is free and supported by ads. Google AdMob may process device and advertising information to serve ads. The app does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA); ads are selected based on context (the content you are viewing). You can manage cross-app tracking in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

3. How Information Is Used

All collected information is used solely to:

  • Provide prayer times, adhan, imsakiye, and Qibla services
  • Send prayer-time and content notifications
  • Share prayer requests with other users, only when you choose to
  • Detect and fix app crashes
  • Show advertisements that keep the app free

Your information is never sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing purposes without your consent.

4. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Identity information such as name, email address, or phone number
  • Payment or billing information (the app is free, with no in-app purchases)
  • Precise GPS history or location logs
  • Contacts, photo library, microphone, or camera access
  • Health, religious affiliation, or biometric data

5. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following external services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Aladhan API, Diyanet (awqatsalah.diyanet.gov.tr), imsakiyem.com: Prayer times and imsakiye calculations; only city/coordinates are sent.
  • alquran.cloud: Quran texts, Mushaf pages, and Ayetel Kürsi audio; only the requested surah/page number is sent.
  • QUD Audio Library (GitHub — mp3quran, tarteel, quranicaudio CDNs): Surah audio in the Yasin and Prayer Surahs sections streams over the internet; a ~5 MB timing package per reciter is downloaded once and cached on the device.
  • archive.org: The Juz listening feature streams its 30 juz audio files from this institutional archive; no files are downloaded.
  • Google Firebase Firestore: Stores the content you voluntarily submit to the Prayer Request feature.
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics: Anonymous crash reports.
  • Google AdMob: Ad delivery. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Quran Foundation (verses.quran.foundation): Mushaf page images.
  • Radio and TV streaming servers: Live broadcasts; only a connection is opened, no viewing log is kept.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play links: For rating the app or opening other apps.

The app does not collect personal data via GitHub, our own servers, or any analytics/tracking SDK.

6. Data Retention

  • Data on your device (city, preferences, downloaded audio): stays until you delete the app.
  • Prayer Request posts: stored in Firebase Firestore until you delete them.
  • Crash reports: anonymous, kept for up to 180 days under Firebase's retention policy.
  • Ad information: kept for limited periods under Google's own policies.

7. Deleting Your Data and Your Rights

You are in full control of your data. Use the options below:

  1. Delete all local data: touch and hold the app icon on your iPhone and tap "Remove App". Your city selection, preferences, notification settings, and all downloaded files are erased from your device.
  2. Delete your Prayer Request content (user data): email our support address below:
    Email: nurserisidestek@gmail.com
    Subject: "Data deletion request"
    Please include your in-app username and, if possible, the first few words of the prayer text you submitted.
    After verification, your content will be deleted within 30 days at the latest, and we will confirm by email. This service is free of charge.
  3. Other rights (access, correction, objection, or restriction requests): send them to the same email address; they are resolved within 30 days.
  4. Ad tracking: no extra permission is needed because IDFA is not used; you may still reset cross-app tracking in iOS Settings > Privacy > Security > Tracking.

8. Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. It offers ad-supported worship and educational content suitable for all ages. If you believe a child has shared personal information with us without your consent, contact us by email and we will remove it promptly.

9. Security

Your settings, city selection, and prayer-tracking data are stored only on your device and are not shared with third parties. Prayer requests you submit are transferred to Firebase solely for in-app publication, over encrypted (TLS) connections. Crash reports are sent only for bug fixing. The app uses the internet for prayer times, Quran texts and audio, content updates, prayer requests, and live broadcasts.

10. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Significant changes will be posted on this page and the "Last updated" date above will be revised. Continuing to use the app constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
