Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026
Türkçe

1. General Information

The Nur Adhan Times & Prayer app is developed by Mehmet Reşat Raz. This privacy policy explains what information is collected when you use our app and how that information is used.

2. Information Collected

Our app uses the following information:

  • Location information: Location access is used only while the app is open, in order to calculate prayer times and the qibla direction. This information stays on your device and is not sent to our servers.
  • Notification permission: Used for adhan time, verse of the day and religious night notifications.
  • Local storage: City selection, theme and settings are stored on your device using SharedPreferences.
  • Prayer requests: The prayer text you submit through the Prayer Request feature, the name you enter (or "Anonymous") and an anonymous device identifier are stored on Google Firebase servers so that other users can see your request and respond to it.

3. Information Not Collected

Our app never collects the following information:

  • Personal identity information such as name, email address or phone number
  • Payment information

4. Third-Party Services

Our app uses the following third-party services:

  • Aladhan API: Used to calculate prayer times. Only location coordinates are sent.
  • GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com): Used for in-app content such as hadith, supplications and radio channels.
  • Google AdMob: Used for the advertisements shown inside the app. The app does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA); the ads shown are selected based on the content you are viewing.
  • Firebase Firestore: Used for the Prayer Request feature. The prayer text you submit, your display name and an anonymous device identifier are stored here and are shown to other users of the app.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Collects crash reports so that app crashes can be detected and fixed.
  • Radio & TV broadcasts: Connects to the relevant broadcast servers for live streaming.

5. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13. The app provides content suitable for all age groups.

6. Security

Your settings, your city selection and your prayer tracking data are stored only on your device and are not shared with third parties. Prayer requests that you voluntarily submit are kept on Google Firebase servers so that other users can see them. Crash reports are sent to Firebase Crashlytics solely to detect and fix problems. The app uses its internet connection for prayer times, content updates, prayer requests and live broadcasts.

7. Changes

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page. Continuing to use the app means that you accept the updated policy.
